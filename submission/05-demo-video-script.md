# Demo video script — 120 seconds (AI@USC allows up to 3:00)

**Setup before recording:** open https://trojan-mentor.vercel.app in a fresh browser window at 1500×1000 or larger. Click **Reset demo data** → **Yes, reset**. Close other tabs and turn off notifications.

| Time | Screen action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Landing page. Slowly scroll past the hero. | "USC's alumni network is huge, but for most students it's a directory they never use. Trojan Mentor turns it into a conversation." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Click **Start the guided demo** → **Open student onboarding**. Tick **Store and process**, then **Continue** → **Use the fictional sample resume**. | "A student gives three separate consents: storing the profile, external AI, and matching. Then they share a resume. Without an AI key, a rule-based parser runs, and we label it *not AI*." |
| 0:25–0:37 | On the review step, point to the label, tick **Tue evening** and **Thu evening**, tick **I've reviewed every field**, then **Save**. | "Nothing is saved until the student reviews every field. The raw resume is never stored." |
| 0:37–0:55 | Click **Re-allocate the cohort**. Pause on the pairings, then scroll to **Not matched this round**. | "Now the whole cohort is optimized at once with min-cost flow, within each mentor's capacity. Every score is explained by real overlaps. Riley is studying marine biology and no mentor fits, so we say so instead of inventing a match." |
| 0:55–1:10 | Open **Maya & Daniel**. Switch **Viewing as** to Maya → **Approve match**. Switch to Daniel → **Approve match**. | "Both sides have to say yes. These roles are simulated for the demo; they're not sign-in." |
| 1:10–1:30 | Switch to **Program coordinator**. **Approve this exact draft**. Set the demo control to **Provider error** → **Send**. Show the failure, then **Retry**. | "Approving a match doesn't send anything. The coordinator approves the exact introduction. Here the email provider fails. The retry reuses the same idempotency key, so there's exactly one delivery, and it's simulated." |
| 1:30–1:45 | Switch to **Maya**. Choose **Tue evening** → **Draft calendar invitation** → **Approve** → **Create**. Then **Confirm the meeting was held** and check two milestones. | "Maya approves an invitation at a time they both have free. After the meeting, her milestones come from her own goals." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Click **Dashboard**. | "The dashboard is computed live from this state: coverage, capacity, approvals, meetings and milestones. Everything here is fictional demo data." |
| 1:55–2:00 | Back to the landing hero. | "Trojan Mentor: your next chapter starts with a conversation." |

**Recording tips:** keep the cursor still while narrating, use 125% browser zoom if text looks small, and record at 1080p or higher. Don't show browser profiles, email or bookmarks.

# Demo video script: 120 seconds (AI@USC allows up to 3:00)

**Before recording:** open https://trojan-mentor.vercel.app in a fresh window at 1500×1000 or larger. Scroll to the footer and click **Reset demo data**, then **Yes, reset**. Download the sample PDF resume from https://trojan-mentor.vercel.app/samples/sample-resume-fictional.pdf. Close other tabs and turn off notifications.

Run matching **before** adding a new student. The original cohort is the one where top-match-first visibly strands a student (425 vs 461).

| Time | Screen action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Landing page. Let the gold line draw between Maya and Daniel. | "USC’s alumni network is huge, but for most students it’s a directory they never use. Trojan Mentor turns it into a conversation." |
| 0:10–0:30 | Click **Open the coordinator view**, then **Cohort**, then **Run allocation**. Pause on the 425 vs 461 comparison, then scroll to **Not matched this round** and **Where more mentors would help**. | "A program coordinator matches the whole cohort at once. Taking top matches first would leave Jordan with no one. Optimizing the whole cohort matches one more student. When there’s no good fit, we say so, and show exactly which alum’s extra place would help." |
| 0:30–0:45 | Open Maya and Daniel’s match. Click **Switch to Maya’s account**, then **Approve match**. Do the same for Daniel. | "Both people have to say yes. Accounts switch freely here because it’s a demo; there’s no real sign-in." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Switch to **Program coordinator** (top right). Read the introduction, click **Approve this exact draft**, set the next attempt to **Fail before sending**, click **Send**, then **Retry**. | "Approving a match doesn’t send anything. The coordinator approves the exact email. If the send fails, the retry uses the same key, so there’s only ever one delivery. Delivery is simulated." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Switch to **Maya**. Pick **Tuesday evenings**, **Draft calendar invitation**, **Approve**, **Create**, then **Confirm the meeting was held** and tick two milestones. Click **Home**. | "Maya approves an invitation at a time they’re both free. After the first meeting, her next steps come from her own goals, and her home shows what’s next." |
| 1:20–1:30 | Switch to **Program coordinator**, open **Dashboard**. | "The coordinator sees the cohort move from suggested to met, counted live. Everyone here is fictional." |
| 1:30–1:52 | In the account menu choose **New student profile…**. Tick **Save my profile**, **Continue**, upload the sample PDF, **Read my resume**. Tick two times and **I’ve checked every detail**, then **Save my profile**. | "New students can join any time. They make three separate choices about their data and upload a resume. Without an AI key, a simple rule-based reader runs, labelled as not AI. Nothing is saved until they check it, and the file is never kept. They land in their own home, in line for the next round." |
| 1:52–2:00 | Back to the landing hero. | "Trojan Mentor: your next chapter starts with a conversation." |

**Recording tips:** keep the cursor still while narrating, use 125% browser zoom if text looks small, and record at 1080p or higher. Don’t show browser profiles, email or bookmarks.
